Inflation

The quiet money leak

What it is

Prices rise over time. So every dollar buys a little less each year.

Median home price, 1960$11,900
Median home price, 2026$393,300
Why it matters

Your money has to grow faster than prices, or you go backward. Retirement can last 30+ years, and your income has to keep up.

Diversification

Don't bet it all on one thing

What it means

Spread your money across things that don't move together: stocks and bonds, big and small companies, different industries, the U.S. and the world.

Why it matters
  • One bad pick can't sink the whole plan.
  • A smoother ride, so you stay in when it gets rough.
  • Something is usually working, even in a bad year.

Asset allocation

How you split it

Asset allocation is your mix of stocks, bonds, etc. Diversification spreads money inside each one.

Stock

You own a slice of a company.

You share in its growth and its drops. More growth over time, bigger swings.

Bond

You lend money to a company or government.

They pay you interest, then pay you back. Steadier, slower growth.

Cash

Savings, money market, CDs.

Safe and ready to use. Often barely keeps up with inflation.
